### Must fix (CORRECTED or NOT FOUND, wording or number is wrong as drafted)

1. **S3 - IAS 38 does not say "data" or "databases".** Para 63 names "brands, mastheads, publishing titles, customer lists and items similar in substance". The word "database" appears zero times in IAS 38 (EU-endorsed text). "Internally generated data is generally not recognised" is an inference by analogy, not a paragraph. Teach it as "para 63 plus para 57 (development criteria); no paragraph addresses data". Also para 20 says subsequent spend on customer lists is expensed even if the list was bought.
2. **S1 (definition) - CISR's definition is about money, not "financial returns".** Book briefing: "Data monetization is the act of turning data into money." The press release uses "financial returns" for the outcome (cost savings, sales, donations, budget). Quote CISR's own sentence.
3. **S1 (three ways) - the third way is worded "selling information offerings" in CISR's briefing.** "Selling information solutions" as a chapter title is UNVERIFIED (MIT Press page was blocked). Use "selling" and the briefing wording.
4. **S1 (numbers) - 51/31/18 are shares of monetisation RETURNS, not revenue and not share of companies.** The revenue figure is a different statistic: top performers 11 percent of revenues vs 2 percent for bottom (CISR 2024 and July 2026 briefings). Do not mix them.
5. **S2 (formulas) - NOT FOUND.** Laney's own slides give the six models as questions, with no formulas. I could not read the book. One patent restates IVI as V*C*(I-S)*L(t) but I could not confirm that is Laney's. Do not print formulas as "Laney's" unless the author has the book.
6. **S5 (anonymisation) - there is no stand-alone "Advisory Guidelines on anonymisation".** It is Chapter 3 "Anonymisation" of the Advisory Guidelines on the PDPA for Selected Topics (revised 23 May 2024).
7. **S5 (business improvement) - cannot cover selling data to unrelated third parties.** PDPC's table limits sharing (collection and disclosure) to a group of related companies. This is an inference from the table (no sentence says "selling is banned"); say so.
8. **S6 (Data Act Arts 8-9) - scope is narrower than "data sold to recipients".** They apply only where a data holder is already obliged to make data available (Art 5 user request or other law), business to business. They are not a general price rule for voluntary data sales. Application is staggered (12 Sep 2025, 12 Sep 2026, 12 Sep 2027).
9. **S8 (Reddit) - $203.0 million is total contract value across several 2 to 3 year deals, not annual, and the S-1 does not name Google.** The $60 million per year figure is a Reuters report citing unnamed sources, not a company disclosure.
10. **S9 - "moved from paid to free" is NOT FOUND in any source I could read.** Free of charge to urban planners, city governments, departments of transportation and safe infrastructure advocates is verified. The earlier paid status is only in search snippets I did not open.
11. **S11 - Pei does not say "high fixed cost".** The word "fixed" appears zero times. The paper says "zero marginal costs" and non-rival. "Tiered" and "usage-based" are also not the paper's terms (it has versioning, bundling, subscription, auctions, query-based pricing, usage-dependent assessment base). Journal citation: IEEE TKDE vol 34 no 10, Oct 2022, pp 4586-4608 (online 2020).
12. **S12 - it is a Gartner prediction, not a measurement, and I found no later measurement.** The only measured figure I read is Gartner's own Oct 2019 baseline: fewer than 50 percent.
