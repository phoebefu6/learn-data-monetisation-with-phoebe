Learn Data Monetisation with Phoebe

Most companies sit on data someone outside would pay for, and most of that data cannot be sold: the privacy notice never covered it, a supplier contract forbids it, or the cells are too small to publish safely. These ten sessions are about the part that can. Four Leader sessions are for the people who decide whether data leaves the building for money: the three roads to data revenue, whether it is yours to sell, products, prices and partners, and the board call. Six Analyst sessions are for the people who build the numbers: three prices for one dataset, the sellable share, cost to serve, pricing a data product, partner deal maths, and a P&L desk that computes all of it live in your browser. The running case, Keelson Mutual's repair cost index, is constructed.
